Sonic

The Sonic movie is full of action, adventure, and comedy. It follows Sonic, a fast blue hedgehog who uses his speed to protect his friends and stop the evil Dr. Robotnik. With fun moments, exciting scenes, and a positive message about friendship and courage, the movie is enjoyable for all ages.
